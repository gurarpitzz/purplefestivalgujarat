### Fees & Exemptions (Action Plan Slides 3, 9, 15)
- **Delegates**: Proposed **₹1,500 for 3 days** (QR code issued on registration for entry; large-print badge & offline scanners as backup).

  - **Note**: Neither `Delegate Registration Form.docx` nor the official Brochure mentions any fee.
- **NGO Stalls**: Registration fee **+ GST**; **lower rate for Organisations of Persons with Disabilities (DPOs)**. Exact rupee tariff not yet published.
- **Pavilions (Corporate / Government / Institutional)**: Registration fee **+ GST**; includes branding and speaking slots. Exact rupee tariff not yet published.
- **GST Gate**: Action Plan explicitly requires confirming GST applicability with the Chartered Accountant (CA) **before publishing any fee**.
- **Volunteers & ISL Interpreters**: No registration fee; **training and certificate for all** (Brochure p. 6). SLI Form 1 asks about honorarium/travel support requirements.
